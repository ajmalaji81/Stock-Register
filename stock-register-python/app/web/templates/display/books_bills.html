How outstanding is worked out
  • Money received or paid settles bills oldest first (FIFO by bill date).
  • A payment taken together with a bill settles that bill first; any extra goes to the oldest bills.
  • The party's opening balance counts as the oldest bill.
  • {{ 'Credit notes, sales returns and receipts settle bills; debit notes add to what is due.' if side == 'receivable' else 'Debit notes, purchase returns and payments settle bills; credit notes add to what is due.' }}
  • Cancelled bills and old-financial-year records are ignored, like the Parties page. Money left over after all bills are settled shows as an advance.
